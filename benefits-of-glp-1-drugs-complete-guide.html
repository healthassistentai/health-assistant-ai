GLP-1 Drugs: More Than Weight Loss

GLP-1 = Glucagon-Like Peptide-1

GLP-1 receptor agonists mimic a natural hormone that helps regulate blood sugar, appetite and digestion.

How They Work:

  • Increase insulin release when needed
  • Reduce excess glucagon
  • Slow stomach emptying
  • Increase feelings of fullness

Better Blood Sugar & Weight Management

Key Benefits:

  • Helps lower blood sugar
  • Improves HbA1c in type 2 diabetes
  • Reduces appetite
  • Supports significant weight loss
  • Helps with long-term weight management

GLP-1 medicines work best alongside a balanced diet and healthy lifestyle.

Benefits Beyond Weight Loss

Potential Health Benefits:

  • ❤️ Heart: May reduce cardiovascular risk
  • 🫘 Kidneys: May help preserve kidney function
  • 🫀 Liver: May reduce liver fat and inflammation
  • 🔥 Inflammation: May support healthier metabolic function

Important: Some benefits are supported by stronger evidence than others, and research is still ongoing.

Who May Benefit From GLP-1 Treatment?

Common Uses:

  • Type 2 diabetes requiring additional treatment
  • Obesity
  • Overweight with certain related health conditions
  • Long-term weight management

Important: GLP-1 medicines are prescription treatments and should be used under medical supervision.

Know the Possible Side Effects

Common Side Effects:

  • Nausea or vomiting
  • Bloating or gas
  • Diarrhea or constipation
  • Reduced appetite
  • Dizziness

Seek Medical Attention For:

  • Severe abdominal pain
  • Persistent vomiting
  • Difficulty breathing or allergic reactions
  • Unusual weakness or serious symptoms

Safety Tip: Never start, stop or change the dose without medical advice.

GLP-1 Works Best With Healthy Habits

Healthy Habits:

  • 🥚 Choose protein-rich foods
  • 🥦 Eat fiber-rich vegetables and fruits
  • 💧 Stay adequately hydrated
  • 🚶 Stay physically active
  • 🏋️ Add appropriate resistance training
