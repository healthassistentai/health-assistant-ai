Gastritis: When Your Stomach Lining Becomes Inflamed

2 main forms: Acute & Chronic Gastritis

Gastritis occurs when the protective lining of the stomach becomes inflamed or irritated.

Common Symptoms of Gastritis

Upper abdominal pain or burning

Nausea or vomiting

Feeling unusually full

Bloating and indigestion

Loss of appetite

Symptoms can range from mild discomfort to persistent pain.

What Can Cause Gastritis?

H. pylori infection

Long-term use of NSAID painkillers

Heavy alcohol use

Severe physical stress

Certain illnesses or autoimmune conditions

The cause of gastritis can vary from person to person.

Gastritis-Friendly Eating

Choose More Often:

  • Oatmeal and other gentle foods
  • Bananas and cooked vegetables
  • Lean proteins
  • Plenty of water
  • Smaller, balanced meals

Limit:

  • Very spicy foods
  • Alcohol
  • Excess caffeine
  • Very fatty or irritating foods

How Is Gastritis Treated?

Treating H. pylori infection

Medicines that reduce stomach acid

Avoiding NSAID painkillers when appropriate

Limiting alcohol and stomach irritants

Following your healthcare professional's advice

Important: Persistent or severe symptoms should be evaluated by a healthcare professional.
