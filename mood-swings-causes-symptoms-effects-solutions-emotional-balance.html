UNDERSTANDING MOOD SWINGS

What Are Mood Swings?

Mood changes can happen to anyone and may be triggered by everyday life events.

Symptoms

  • Sudden happiness or sadness
  • Irritability or anger
  • Emotional sensitivity
  • Rapid changes in feelings

Causes

  • Stress
  • Hormonal changes
  • Lack of sleep
  • Life events

Prevention Tips

  • Maintain a regular sleep schedule
  • Manage stress
  • Stay physically active

WHAT CAUSES MOOD SWINGS

What Causes Mood Swings?

Mood changes can have emotional, physical, hormonal, or lifestyle-related causes.

Symptoms

  • Emotional ups and downs
  • Restlessness
  • Irritability
  • Difficulty concentrating

Causes

  • Hormonal changes
  • Stress
  • Genetics
  • Caffeine
  • Sleep problems
  • Major life changes

Prevention Tips

  • Get enough sleep
  • Limit excessive caffeine
  • Practice relaxation techniques

COMMON SYMPTOMS

Common Symptoms of Mood Swings

Mood swings may affect emotions, behavior, sleep, and daily activities.

Symptoms

  • Sudden irritability
  • Anxiety or sadness
  • Feeling overwhelmed
  • Trouble sleeping
  • Low energy
  • Difficulty focusing

Causes

  • Stress
  • Sleep deprivation
  • Hormonal fluctuations
  • Emotional pressure

Prevention Tips

  • Track mood changes
  • Maintain healthy routines
  • Talk to someone you trust

EMOTIONAL & MENTAL HEALTH EFFECTS

Emotional & Mental Health Effects

Persistent or severe mood changes can interfere with relationships and everyday life.

Symptoms

  • Emotional exhaustion
  • Persistent sadness
  • Anxiety or worry
  • Social withdrawal
  • Negative thoughts
  • Reduced motivation

Causes

  • Chronic stress
  • Ongoing sleep problems
  • Relationship difficulties
  • Underlying mental health conditions

Prevention Tips

  • Seek support early
  • Reduce prolonged stress
  • Maintain social connections

MANAGING MOOD SWINGS

Effective Ways to Manage Mood Swings

Healthy lifestyle habits can support emotional well-being and mood stability.

Symptoms

  • Stress and tension
  • Poor sleep
  • Low energy
  • Emotional instability

Causes

  • Irregular sleep
  • Physical inactivity
  • Poor stress management
  • Unhealthy routines

Prevention Tips

  • Sleep 7–9 hours regularly
  • Exercise consistently
  • Practice meditation or breathing exercises
  • Eat a balanced diet
  • Stay socially connected
