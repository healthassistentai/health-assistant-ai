UNDERSTANDING THE CONDITION

What is High Blood Pressure?

Nearly 1 in 3 adults worldwide suffer from hypertension.

Watch for

  • Often called the "silent killer" with no obvious signs
  • Occasional morning headaches or mild dizziness in some cases
  • Shortness of breath during minor physical exertion

Why it happens

  • Excess sodium intake in daily diet
  • Lack of regular physical activity and sedentary lifestyle
  • Chronic stress and genetic predisposition

Try this

  • Check your blood pressure regularly at home or a clinic
  • Reduce daily salt and processed food consumption

DASH DIET

Fuel Your Body with the DASH Diet

Following a potassium-rich diet can reduce systolic pressure by up to 10 mmHg.

Watch for

  • Fatigue caused by poor nutritional intake
  • Sluggish digestion affecting overall vitality

Why it happens

  • High consumption of saturated fats and refined sugars
  • Insufficient intake of fresh fruits and vegetables

Try this

  • Fill half your plate with leafy greens and colorful vegetables
  • Choose whole grains like brown rice and oats over refined flour

STRESS REDUCTION

Calm Your Mind, Lower Your Pressure

Chronic stress keeps cortisol elevated, tightening blood vessels over time.

Watch for

  • Persistent tension headaches and jaw clenching
  • Inability to focus or racing thoughts

Why it happens

  • Overworking and high-pressure career environments
  • Lack of personal downtime and relaxation habits

Try this

  • Practice 10 minutes of deep breathing or meditation daily
  • Spend time in nature to naturally lower stress hormones

REGULAR EXERCISE

Move More for a Stronger Heart

Regular aerobic exercise can lower blood pressure by 5 to 8 mmHg.

Watch for

  • Decreased stamina and endurance during simple tasks
  • Muscle stiffness due to prolonged sitting

Why it happens

  • Prolonged sitting or standing without movement
  • Weakened cardiovascular endurance

Try this

  • Aim for at least 30 minutes of brisk walking daily
  • Incorporate light stretching into your morning routine

SLEEP QUALITY

Prioritize Restful Sleep

Sleeping less than 6 hours a night increases your risk of chronic hypertension.

Watch for

  • Daytime grogginess and chronic exhaustion
  • Irritability and mood swings due to fatigue

Why it happens

  • Irregular sleep schedules and late-night screen time
  • Sleep apnea or untreated nighttime breathing issues

Try this

  • Maintain a consistent sleep and wake schedule every day
  • Keep your bedroom dark, cool, and free of digital devices

MONITORING & HYDRATION

Stay Hydrated & Track Your Progress

Proper hydration helps blood vessels maintain optimal elasticity.

Watch for

  • Dry mouth and frequent dehydration headaches
  • Sudden lightheadedness when standing up

Why it happens

  • Inadequate water intake throughout the day
  • Excessive consumption of caffeinated or sugary beverages

Try this

  • Drink at least 8 glasses of water daily
  • Keep a log of your weekly blood pressure readings
