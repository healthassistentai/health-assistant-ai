BERRIES

The Power of Berries: Nature's Anti-Inflammatory

Berries contain high levels of anthocyanins, which can reduce inflammatory markers by up to 20-30% according to some studies.

Symptoms of chronic inflammation

  • Chronic Fatigue and Insomnia
  • Body Aches and Joint Pain
  • Digestive Issues (e.g., bloating, discomfort)
  • Skin Issues (e.g., psoriasis, acne)

Contributing causes

  • Poor Diet (Processed Foods, High Sugar)
  • Chronic Stress
  • Lack of Physical Activity
  • Environmental Toxins

Prevention tips

  • Increase intake of colorful fruits and vegetables
  • Manage stress levels through meditation or yoga
  • Prioritize sleep (7-9 hours per night)
  • Limit intake of red and processed meats

OMEGA-3 FATTY ACIDS

Omega-3 Fatty Acids: The Key to Joint Health

Oily fish like salmon provides 1,500-2,000 mg of EPA/DHA per 3.5-ounce serving, crucial for reducing inflammatory eicosanoids.

Symptoms

  • Joint Stiffness and Swelling
  • Reduced Mobility or Range of Motion
  • Tender Joints
  • "Brain Fog" and Memory Issues

Causes

  • High Intake of Pro-inflammatory Omega-6s (Vegetable Oils)
  • Smoking
  • Excessive Alcohol Consumption
  • Sedentary Lifestyle

Prevention tips

  • Consume fatty fish at least twice a week
  • Supplement with high-quality Omega-3s if needed
  • Reduce consumption of processed vegetable oils (corn, soybean, sunflower)
  • Quit smoking

CRUCIFEROUS VEGETABLES

Cruciferous Vegetables & Carotenoids: Your Body's Defense

Eating 3-4 servings of cruciferous vegetables per week is linked to a significantly lower risk of chronic inflammatory diseases.

Symptoms

  • Unintentional Weight Gain (especially abdominal)
  • Frequent Infections or Illness
  • Gum Disease (Periodontitis)
  • Constant Low-grade Fever

Causes

  • Low Dietary Fiber Intake
  • Poor Gut Health (Dysbiosis)
  • Autoimmune Reactions
  • Chronic Infection

Prevention tips

  • Make half your plate non-starchy vegetables
  • Include a wide variety of colors to get different phytonutrients
  • Focus on gut health with pre- and probiotics
  • Ensure adequate hydration

NUTS, SEEDS & HEALTHY FATS

Nuts, Seeds, and Healthy Fats: Building a Stronger Body

Regular consumption of nuts (like walnuts) and seeds (like chia/flax) can lower CRP (C-reactive protein), a marker of inflammation, by 15-20%.

Symptoms

  • Dry Eyes and Mouth
  • Depression and Anxiety
  • Chest Pain
  • Rashes or Skin Disorders

Causes

  • Insulin Resistance and Diabetes
  • Obesity
  • Genetic Predisposition
  • Nutrient Deficiencies

Prevention tips

  • Enjoy a small handful of nuts/seeds daily
  • Use avocado or extra virgin olive oil as your primary fat source
  • Maintain a healthy weight
  • Monitor blood sugar levels

TURMERIC, GINGER & OLIVE OIL

Golden Medicine: Turmeric, Ginger, and Olive Oil

Curcumin, the active compound in Turmeric, has anti-inflammatory power comparable to some pharmaceutical drugs, but without the side effects.

Symptoms

  • Swollen Lymph Nodes
  • Elevated Blood Sugar
  • High Blood Pressure
  • General Malaise or Feeling Unwell

Causes

  • Aging
  • Hormonal Imbalances
  • Poor Dental Hygiene
  • Chronic Injury or Trauma

Prevention tips

  • Add turmeric and ginger to your cooking daily
  • Pair turmeric with black pepper to increase absorption by 2000%
  • Use extra virgin olive oil for low-heat cooking and dressings
  • Get regular health check-ups

WHOLE GRAINS & LEGUMES

Whole Grains and Legumes: Fueling Recovery

Swapping refined grains for whole grains like quinoa can reduce inflammatory markers, such as interleukin-6 (IL-6), by up to 30%.

Symptoms

  • Visible Swelling or Edema
  • Redness and Heat in Joints
  • Chronic Pain
  • Irritable Bowel Syndrome (IBS) Symptoms

Causes

  • Chronic Sleep Deprivation
  • High Stress Lifestyle
  • Nutrient-poor Diet
  • Environmental Pollution

Prevention tips

  • Choose whole grains (quinoa, brown rice, oats) over refined ones
  • Incorporate legumes (beans, lentils, chickpeas) into your meals
  • Manage stress effectively
  • Prioritize sleep hygiene
