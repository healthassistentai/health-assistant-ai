Proper Handwashing

Handwashing with soap cuts diarrheal disease by 23–40% and respiratory illness by 16–21%.

Watch for

  • Frequent stomach infections or diarrhea
  • Repeated colds and coughs
  • Skin rashes or infections on hands

Why it happens

  • Germs from toilets, surfaces, and food
  • Not using soap or washing under 20 seconds
  • Touching your face with dirty hands

Do this

  • Wet hands, apply soap, scrub 20+ seconds
  • Clean between fingers, nails, and hand backs
  • Rinse well and dry with a clean towel

Wash your hands the right way every day – protect yourself and your family!

Brush Your Teeth Twice Daily

Proper brushing reduces plaque by 50–60% and lowers gingivitis risk by 21%.

Watch for

  • Bad breath
  • Bleeding gums
  • Tooth sensitivity or cavities

Why it happens

  • Plaque buildup from food and bacteria
  • Brushing less than 2 minutes
  • Skipping fluoride toothpaste

Do this

  • Brush 2 full minutes, twice a day
  • Use soft bristles and fluoride toothpaste
  • Clean outer, inner, and chewing surfaces

Spend just 2 minutes twice a day for a healthier smile!

Keep Your Nails Clean

Dirty nails can carry parasites and bacteria – up to 19% of nail samples may contain harmful organisms.

Watch for

  • Nail infections or paronychia
  • Skin irritation around nails
  • Transfer of germs to mouth or food

Why it happens

  • Long or uncleaned nails trapping dirt
  • Not scrubbing under nails while washing
  • Contact with contaminated surfaces

Do this

  • Keep nails short and clean
  • Scrub under nails with a soft brush daily
  • Wash hands after outdoor activities

Clean nails = cleaner hands. Make it a daily habit!

Wash Your Face Twice a Day

Daily face cleansing removes dirt, oil, and pollutants that clog pores and cause skin problems.

Watch for

  • Acne or breakouts
  • Dull, oily, or irritated skin
  • Clogged pores

Why it happens

  • Excess oil, sweat, and environmental dirt
  • Skipping cleanser and using only water
  • Over-washing or harsh products

Do this

  • Wash morning and night with a gentle cleanser
  • Use lukewarm water, soft circular motions
  • Moisturize after washing

Clean face, clear skin – start your twice-daily routine today!

Wash Your Hair Regularly

Washing hair 5–6 times a week improves scalp health and reduces flaking, itch, and oil buildup.

Watch for

  • Itchy or flaky scalp
  • Greasy or dull hair
  • Scalp odor or irritation

Why it happens

  • Buildup of sebum, sweat, and dirt
  • Washing too infrequently
  • Not rinsing shampoo thoroughly

Do this

  • Wash based on your hair type (every 1–2 days)
  • Focus on cleansing the scalp gently
  • Rinse fully, condition the ends

Healthy scalp, healthy hair – wash regularly for best results!

Don’t Forget to Floss

Flossing removes plaque from between teeth that brushing alone can’t reach, helping prevent gum disease.

Watch for

  • Bleeding or swollen gums
  • Food stuck between teeth
  • Persistent bad breath

Why it happens

  • Plaque and food debris between teeth
  • Skipping flossing completely
  • Incorrect flossing technique

Do this

  • Floss at least once a day, at night
  • Gently slide floss in a C-shape
  • Clean both sides of each tooth

Brush + Floss = Complete oral care. Start tonight!
