INTRODUCTION

Iron and Vitamin B12: The Ultimate Energy Duo

Over 30% of the global population suffers from anemia or micronutrient deficiencies.

Watch for

  • Chronic fatigue and constant exhaustion
  • Dizziness, lightheadedness, and frequent headaches
  • Pale skin and brittle nails

Why it happens

  • Inadequate dietary intake of iron and B12
  • Poor nutrient absorption in the gut
  • Increased bodily demand during growth or stress

Try this

  • Eat a balanced diet rich in whole foods
  • Combine plant-based iron with Vitamin C for better absorption
  • Consult a doctor for routine blood tests

ANIMAL PROTEINS

Power-Packed Animal Proteins

Just 3 ounces of beef provides over 50% of your daily recommended Vitamin B12 intake.

Watch for

  • Muscle weakness and physical fatigue
  • Cold hands and feet due to poor circulation
  • Brain fog and difficulty concentrating

Why it happens

  • Strict plant-based diets without proper supplementation
  • Low stomach acid reducing B12 extraction

Try this

  • Include lean meats, poultry, and eggs in moderation
  • Choose grass-fed or organic options when possible

LEGUMES AND BEANS

Plant-Powered Legumes & Beans

One cup of cooked lentils provides about 37% of your daily iron requirement.

Watch for

  • Shortness of breath during mild physical activity
  • Decreased exercise endurance and slow recovery

Why it happens

  • Relying heavily on processed foods lacking essential minerals
  • Presence of phytates in beans that block iron absorption

Try this

  • Soak or sprout beans before cooking to enhance mineral absorption
  • Pair legumes with citrus fruits or bell peppers (Vitamin C)

LEAFY GREENS AND SEEDS

Vibrant Greens & Nutrient-Dense Seeds

A single cup of cooked spinach contains approximately 6.4 mg of iron.

Watch for

  • Hair thinning and excessive hair fall
  • Dry, irritated skin and cracked mouth corners

Why it happens

  • Consuming tea or coffee immediately with meals (inhibits iron uptake)

Try this

  • Add pumpkin seeds or hemp seeds to your daily salads
  • Steam or lightly cook greens to reduce oxalates

FORTIFIED FOODS

Smart Fortified Foods for Everyone

Fortified cereals and nutritional yeast can supply 100% of your daily B12 value in a single serving.

Watch for

  • Tingling sensations or numbness in hands and feet
  • Mood swings, irritability, and mild anxiety

Why it happens

  • Total avoidance of animal products without checking alternative nutrient labels

Try this

  • Read nutrition labels carefully to find fortified plant milks and cereals
  • Incorporate nutritional yeast into soups and sauces

SEAFOOD

Nutrient-Rich Seafood Selection

Clams contain one of the highest concentrations of Vitamin B12 and heme iron found in nature.

Watch for

  • Rapid heartbeat or heart palpitations during rest
  • Weakened immune system leading to frequent infections

Why it happens

  • Irregular eating habits and lack of diverse nutrient sources

Try this

  • Include fish like salmon or tuna in your weekly meal plan
  • Ensure seafood is sustainably sourced and freshly prepared
