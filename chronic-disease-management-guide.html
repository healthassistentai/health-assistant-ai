INTRODUCTION & OVERVIEW

Chronic Disease Management Guide

60% of adults live with at least one chronic condition globally.

Watch for

  • Persistent fatigue and low energy levels
  • Unexplained weight fluctuations
  • Chronic, low-grade body aches

Why it happens

  • Genetic predispositions and family history
  • Sedentary lifestyle and lack of physical activity
  • Chronic stress and poor sleep patterns

Try this

  • Schedule routine annual health checkups
  • Maintain a balanced, nutrient-rich diet
  • Prioritize 7-8 hours of quality sleep nightly

NUTRITION & LIFESTYLE

Nutrition and Lifestyle Medicine

50% of chronic conditions are preventable via lifestyle changes.

Watch for

  • Digestive discomfort and bloating
  • Sluggish metabolism and weight gain
  • Frequent nutritional deficiencies

Why it happens

  • High consumption of processed foods and sugars
  • Chronic dehydration and poor fluid intake
  • Vitamin and mineral deficiencies

Try this

  • Eat a rainbow of whole fruits and vegetables
  • Drink at least 8 glasses of water daily
  • Eliminate processed sugars and trans fats

Movement as Medicine

30 minutes of daily exercise cuts disease risk by 40%.

Watch for

  • Joint stiffness and muscle weakness

Why it happens

  • Prolonged sitting or desk jobs
