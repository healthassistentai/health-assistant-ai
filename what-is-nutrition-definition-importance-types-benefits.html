WHOLE FOODS

The Foundation of Wellness: Whole Foods

Over 90% of adults do not meet the recommended daily intake of vegetables. (Source: CDC)

Symptoms

  • Lethargy due to poor diet
  • Weakened immune system
  • Skin dullness

Causes

  • Low availability of fresh produce
  • High consumption of processed foods
  • Lack of dietary knowledge

Prevention Tips

  • Incorporate at least 5 servings of fruits and vegetables daily
  • Choose a "rainbow" of colors for diverse nutrients
  • Opt for whole, unprocessed options whenever possible

GUT HEALTH

Optimizing Your Gut Health

An estimated 60-70 million people in the US are affected by digestive diseases. (Source: NIDDK)

Symptoms

  • Bloating and Gas
  • Abdominal Pain
  • Irregular Bowel Movements

Causes

  • Poor fiber intake
  • Processed and high-fat foods
  • Stress and gut microbiome imbalance

Prevention Tips

  • Consume adequate dietary fiber
  • Stay hydrated
  • Include probiotics in your diet

BALANCED MEAL

Mastering the Balanced Meal

Only 1 in 10 Americans meet the daily recommendations for fiber and potassium. (Source: USDA Dietary Guidelines)

Symptoms

  • Persistent fatigue
  • Unintentional weight changes
  • Nutrient deficiencies (e.g., anemia)

Causes

  • Lack of nutritional planning
  • Reliance on fast food
  • Imbalanced macronutrient intake

Prevention Tips

  • Adopt the "MyPlate" method for portion control
  • Balance proteins, carbohydrates, and fats
  • Focus on nutrient density, not just calories

ACTIVE LIFESTYLE

Fueling Your Physical Activity

Insufficient physical activity is a key risk factor for chronic diseases like obesity and heart disease. (Source: WHO)

Symptoms

  • Poor stamina and endurance
  • Muscle weakness
  • Slow post-exercise recovery

Causes

  • Sedentary lifestyle
  • Inadequate pre- and post-workout nutrition
  • Lack of motivation

Prevention Tips

  • Engage in regular exercise (e.g., 150 mins moderate activity/week)
  • Hydrate before, during, and after exercise
  • Replenish electrolytes and protein after workouts

VITALITY & HEALTH

The Look of Vitality and Health

Individuals who eat a healthy diet have a 20% lower mortality rate from all causes. (Source: The Lancet)

Symptoms

  • Dull complexion
  • Brittle hair and nails
  • Low energy levels

Causes

  • Chronic nutrient deficiency
  • Dehydration
  • High sugar consumption

Prevention Tips

  • Ensure a wide variety of vitamins and minerals in the diet
  • Maintain optimal hydration
  • Manage stress to improve physical appearance

HEALTHY HABITS FOR LIFE

Building Healthy Habits for Life

An estimated 38.2 million children under the age of 5 were overweight or obese in 2019. (Source: WHO)

Symptoms

  • Early onset of chronic conditions
  • Poor childhood development
  • Inconsistent energy throughout the day

Causes

  • Generational eating patterns
  • Marketing of unhealthy foods to children
  • Busy family schedules leading to poor choices

Prevention Tips

  • Make healthy eating a family priority
  • Involve children in meal planning and cooking
  • Promote healthy choices at home and school
