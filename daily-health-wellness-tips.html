Start Your Day With Mindfulness

10 minutes of mindfulness can help you feel more calm and focused.

Watch for

  • Feeling stressed or overwhelmed
  • Difficulty concentrating
  • Restlessness during the day

Common causes

  • Busy daily routines
  • Excessive screen time
  • Poor stress management

Try this

  • Take 5–10 minutes for quiet breathing
  • Practice mindfulness regularly
  • Start your morning without your phone

Pause. Breathe. Reset Your Mind.

Build a Healthier Plate

Aim to fill about half your plate with vegetables and fruits.

Watch for

  • Low energy
  • Frequent hunger
  • Difficulty maintaining a balanced diet

Common causes

  • Highly processed foods
  • Too few fruits and vegetables
  • Irregular meal choices

Try this

  • Add colorful vegetables to meals
  • Choose whole grains and protein
  • Limit highly processed foods

Make Your Next Meal Count.

Move More, Feel Better

Aim for at least 150 minutes of moderate physical activity each week.

Watch for

  • Low energy
  • Stiffness after sitting
  • Reduced physical activity

Common causes

  • Long hours of sitting
  • Sedentary lifestyle
  • Lack of regular exercise

Try this

  • Walk or exercise regularly
  • Take short movement breaks
  • Choose activities you enjoy

Get Up. Get Moving. Stay Active.

Make Quality Sleep a Priority

Most adults need 7 or more hours of sleep each night.

Watch for

  • Daytime tiredness
  • Difficulty concentrating
  • Feeling unrefreshed after sleep

Common causes

  • Irregular sleep schedule
  • Late-night screen use
  • Stress before bedtime

Try this

  • Keep a consistent sleep schedule
  • Create a relaxing bedtime routine
  • Reduce screen use before bed

Sleep Well. Wake Up Ready.

Stay Hydrated Throughout the Day

Your daily fluid needs vary with activity, weather, diet, and health.

Watch for

  • Dry mouth
  • Darker-than-usual urine
  • Feeling thirsty or tired

Common causes

  • Not drinking enough fluids
  • Hot weather
  • Exercise and sweating

Try this

  • Keep water nearby
  • Drink regularly throughout the day
  • Increase fluids during exercise or heat

Sip Smart. Stay Hydrated.

Give Your Mind Time to Relax

Even short relaxation breaks can help you reset during a busy day.

Watch for

  • Mental fatigue
  • Feeling tense
  • Difficulty switching off

Common causes

  • Continuous work or study
  • Too much screen time
  • Lack of regular breaks

Try this

  • Take short screen-free breaks
  • Listen to calming music
  • Spend time outdoors
  • Practice slow, deep breathing
