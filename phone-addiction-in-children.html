Early Warning Signs

1 in 4 children aged 8-12 has a smartphone.
  • Becomes anxious or irritable if the phone is taken away
  • Hides use, e.g. under blankets or at night
  • Neglects homework, chores, and personal hygiene
  • Loses interest in offline hobbies and activities
  • Easy access to content
  • Fear of Missing Out (FOMO)
  • Apps designed to be addictive
Set Healthy Limits Today

Relationships & Social Isolation

Families spend 20% less face-to-face time than 20 years ago.
40% of children feel lonely even when connected online.
  • Lack of eye contact during conversations
  • Reduced quality of family bonding
  • Difficulty making real-world friends
  • Increased social anxiety and cyberbullying exposure
Build Real-World Connections

Sleep Disruption

72% of children keep a device in their bedroom at night.
  • Chronic sleep deprivation and fatigue
  • Difficulty falling asleep (insomnia)
  • Poor concentration and mood swings
  • Irregular sleep schedules
  • Blue light exposure suppresses melatonin
  • Engaging content keeps kids up late
Prioritize Restful Sleep

Academic & Cognitive Effects

Heavy phone users have GPA scores 0.5-1 point lower.
  • Constant notifications disrupt deep work
  • Shortened attention span
  • Poor academic performance and grades
  • Difficulty with long-term projects and focus
  • Reduced creativity and problem-solving skills

The Path to Balance

88% of parents see improved behavior with reduced screen time.
  • Model healthy digital habits as parents
  • Plan active, screen-free family outings
  • Introduce offline hobbies: art, music, sports
  • Use parental control software for guided access
