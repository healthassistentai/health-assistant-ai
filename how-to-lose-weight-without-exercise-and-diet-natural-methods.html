  • Sugar cravings
  • Low energy

WHY IT HAPPENS

Sleep & Weight Management: Common Causes

  • Insufficient sleep
  • Irregular sleep schedule
  • Too much screen time at night
  • Late-night caffeine

Prevention Tips
