Understanding Psychiatric Illness

Nearly 1 in 7 people worldwide live with a mental disorder.

Mental disorders can affect emotions, thoughts, behavior, relationships, and everyday functioning.

What Happens in Mental Illness?

Persistent anxiety or fear

Low mood or loss of interest

Changes in sleep or appetite

Difficulty concentrating

Unusual thoughts or perceptions

Problems with daily functioning

Symptoms vary widely depending on the condition and the individual.

Recognize the Warning Signs

Feeling overwhelmed for long periods

Withdrawal from family or friends

Major changes in mood or behavior

Persistent hopelessness

Difficulty managing daily activities

Thoughts of self-harm or suicide

Don't Ignore Persistent Symptoms

Seek professional help when symptoms are persistent, severe, or interfere with daily life.

What Can Cause Psychiatric Illness?

Genetic and biological factors

Traumatic or stressful experiences

Family and relationship difficulties

Substance use

Poverty and social disadvantage

Violence, abuse, or discrimination

Mental disorders usually result from a complex interaction of biological, psychological, social, and environmental factors.

Recovery Is Possible

Build strong social connections

Stay physically active

Maintain healthy routines

Learn healthy coping skills

Talk to someone you trust

Seek professional help early

Effective psychological and medical treatments are available for many mental disorders.

Protect Your Mental Health

Prioritize regular sleep

Exercise regularly

Stay connected with others

Manage stress effectively

Avoid harmful substance use

Ask for help when needed
