Build a Healthy Lifestyle

150 minutes of moderate physical activity per week is a common guideline for adults.

Common Signs

  • Low energy
  • Poor mood
  • Reduced fitness
  • Feeling inactive

Possible Causes

  • Sedentary lifestyle
  • Too little physical activity
  • Poor daily routine

Prevention Tips

  • Walk or exercise regularly
  • Stay active throughout the day
  • Spend time outdoors
  • Maintain a consistent routine

Start moving today for a healthier tomorrow.

Fuel Your Body with Healthy Food

A balanced diet provides essential nutrients your body needs every day.

Common Signs

  • Low energy
  • Poor concentration
  • Digestive discomfort
  • Unbalanced nutrition

Possible Causes

  • Highly processed foods
  • Low fruit and vegetable intake
  • Irregular eating habits

Prevention Tips

  • Eat more vegetables and fruits
  • Choose whole foods
  • Drink enough water
  • Maintain balanced meals

Choose nutritious foods to nourish your body every day.

Sleep Well, Live Well

Most adults need at least 7 hours of sleep each night.

Common Signs

  • Daytime tiredness
  • Poor concentration
  • Low mood
  • Reduced alertness

Possible Causes

  • Irregular sleep schedule
  • Excessive screen time at night
  • Stress and poor sleep habits

Prevention Tips

  • Follow a regular sleep schedule
  • Create a relaxing bedtime routine
  • Limit screens before bed
  • Keep your bedroom comfortable and quiet

Make quality sleep a priority every night.

Protect Your Mental Well-Being

Even a few minutes of mindfulness each day can become part of a healthy stress-management routine.

Common Signs

  • Feeling stressed
  • Restlessness
  • Difficulty relaxing
  • Mental fatigue

Possible Causes

  • Daily stress
  • Lack of relaxation
  • Poor work-life balance
  • Constant digital stimulation

Prevention Tips

  • Practice mindfulness
  • Take regular breaks
  • Spend time in nature
  • Maintain healthy social connections

Pause, breathe, and give your mind time to recharge.

Take Care of Your Heart

Regular physical activity, a balanced diet, and avoiding tobacco can help support cardiovascular health.

Common Signs

  • Unusual fatigue
  • Reduced exercise tolerance
  • Shortness of breath
  • Chest discomfort

Possible Causes

  • Physical inactivity
  • Unhealthy eating patterns
  • Smoking or tobacco use
  • Poor lifestyle habits

Prevention Tips

  • Stay physically active
  • Eat a heart-friendly diet
  • Avoid tobacco
  • Monitor blood pressure and cholesterol

Take small steps today to protect your heart health.

Balance Health, Work & Life

Healthy living works best when nutrition, movement, sleep, mental well-being, and social connection are balanced.

Common Signs

  • Constant stress
  • Burnout
  • Low motivation
  • Poor work-life balance

Possible Causes

  • Overworking
  • Lack of rest
  • Neglecting personal time
  • Unhealthy daily routines

Prevention Tips

  • Set healthy boundaries
  • Make time for family and friends
  • Schedule regular breaks
  • Prioritize sleep and exercise
  • Enjoy meaningful activities

Create a healthier balance and make wellness part of your everyday life.
