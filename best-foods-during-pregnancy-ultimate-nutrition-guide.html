LEAFY GREENS

Power Up with Leafy Greens

90% of pregnant women can benefit from increased daily folate intake to prevent neural tube defects.

Combats fatigue, prevents anemia, and supports early embryonic development.

Key Points

  • Rich in natural folate, iron, and calcium
  • Packed with fiber to promote healthy digestion
  • Easy to add to daily salads, smoothies, or soups

Over 150% of your daily Vitamin C requirement can be met with just one cup of cooked broccoli.

Reduces inflammation, supports cellular growth, and boosts natural immunity.

DAIRY & CALCIUM SOURCES

Build Strong Bones with Dairy

1,000 mg of calcium is recommended daily during pregnancy to support developing bones and teeth.

Prevents maternal bone density loss and supports fetal skeletal growth.

Key Points

  • Excellent source of highly absorbable calcium and protein
  • Contains gut-friendly probiotics (in yogurt) for digestion
  • Supports muscle and nerve function for mom and baby

LEAN RED MEAT & LENTILS

Fight Fatigue with Iron-Rich Foods

Blood volume increases by nearly 50% during pregnancy, drastically raising iron demands.

Prevents gestational anemia, dizziness, and extreme pregnancy fatigue.

Key Points

  • Packed with bioavailable heme iron and protein
  • Combines plant-based lentils with lean beef for sustained energy
  • Supports optimal oxygen delivery throughout the body

Fuel Your Day with Plant Protein & Fiber
