Are You Ageing Faster Than Your Birth Age?

Chronological age and biological age are not always the same.

Your birth certificate shows your chronological age. Your biological age reflects how your body is ageing internally.

Lifestyle, environment and genetics can influence the pace of ageing.

What Happens Inside Your Cells?

Oxidative stress can damage cells
Free radicals can affect DNA and proteins
Cellular damage accumulates over time
Mitochondrial changes can affect energy production

Ageing is a complex biological process involving changes across cells, tissues and organs.
