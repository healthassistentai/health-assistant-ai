INTRODUCTION

What Are Infectious Diseases?

Germs are everywhere, but not all germs cause disease.

Bullet Points

  • Caused by harmful microorganisms
  • Can spread between people
  • May range from mild to severe

Cause

  • Viruses
  • Bacteria
  • Fungi
  • Parasites

Prevention

  • Good hygiene
  • Vaccination
  • Safe food and water

COMMON SYMPTOMS

Common Symptoms

Symptoms can vary depending on the type of infection.

Symptoms

  • Fever or chills
  • Cough and sore throat
  • Fatigue and weakness
  • Headache or body aches
  • Vomiting or diarrhea

Causes

  • Viral infections
  • Bacterial infections
  • Contaminated food or water

Prevention

  • Wash hands
  • Cover coughs
  • Avoid close contact when sick

PREVENTION

How to Prevent Infections

Handwashing can help reduce the spread of respiratory and diarrheal infections.

Prevention Tips

  • Wash hands with soap
  • Keep vaccinations up to date
  • Drink safe water
  • Eat properly cooked food
  • Practice respiratory hygiene

Causes to Avoid

  • Poor hygiene
  • Unsafe food
  • Unsafe water
  • Infected-person exposure

TREATMENT & RECOVERY

Treatment & Recovery

Treatment depends on the specific cause of the infection.

Treatment

  • Antibiotics for some bacterial infections
  • Antivirals for some viral infections
  • Antifungals for fungal infections
  • Antiparasitic medicines for parasites
  • Rest and fluids when appropriate

Important: Antibiotics do not treat viral infections.

Prevention

  • Follow medical advice
  • Take medicines correctly
  • Avoid self-medication
