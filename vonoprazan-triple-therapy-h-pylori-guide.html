H. pylori: The Hidden Stomach Infection

Helicobacter pylori is a bacterium that can live in the stomach lining and contribute to gastritis and peptic ulcers.

Symptoms

Stomach burning
Indigestion
Bloating
Persistent stomach discomfort
Testing is important before starting eradication treatment.

How Does Triple Therapy Work?

Vonoprazan strongly suppresses stomach acid
Antibiotics target H. pylori bacteria
Combined treatment aims to eradicate the infection
