Symptoms / Signs of Low Hydration

  • Dry mouth
  • Dark-colored urine
  • Fatigue
  • Headache
  • Dizziness

Possible Causes

  • Not drinking enough water
  • Excessive sweating
  • Hot weather
  • Intense physical activity

Prevention Tips

  • Drink water regularly
  • Include water-rich foods like cucumber
  • Stay hydrated during hot weather
  • Eat a balanced diet

Calcium-Rich Foods for Stronger Bones

Calcium is the most abundant mineral in the human body.

Symptoms of Low Calcium Intake

  • Muscle cramps
  • Tingling or numbness
  • Weak or brittle bones
  • Increased risk of fractures

Possible Causes

  • Low-calcium diet
  • Vitamin D deficiency
  • Poor nutrient absorption
  • Certain medical conditions

Prevention Tips

  • Include dairy or fortified alternatives
  • Eat leafy green vegetables
  • Add calcium-rich foods to regular meals
  • Get adequate vitamin D
Build Strong Bones Through Better Nutrition.

Protect Your Bones at Every Age

Bone health depends on calcium, vitamin D, protein, and regular physical activity.

Warning Signs

  • Frequent fractures
  • Back pain
  • Loss of height
  • Weakness
  • Reduced mobility

Possible Causes

  • Low calcium or vitamin D
  • Physical inactivity
  • Aging
  • Smoking
  • Poor nutrition

Prevention Tips

  • Eat calcium-rich foods
  • Maintain adequate vitamin D
  • Do weight-bearing exercise
  • Avoid smoking
  • Maintain a healthy body weight

A Balanced Diet Supports Better Health

A healthy diet should provide a variety of essential nutrients from different food groups.

Signs of Poor Nutrition

  • Low energy
  • Weakness
  • Poor concentration
  • Unhealthy weight changes
  • Slow recovery

Possible Causes

  • Highly processed foods
  • Skipping meals
  • Limited food variety
  • Insufficient fruits and vegetables

Prevention Tips

  • Eat a variety of whole foods
  • Include vegetables and fruits
  • Choose healthy protein sources
  • Include whole grains
  • Limit highly processed foods
Small Nutrition Changes Can Make a Big Difference.

Healthy Habits Build a Healthier Life

Regular physical activity supports bone, muscle, heart, and overall health.

Signs You May Need More Activity

  • Low stamina
  • Muscle weakness
  • Stiffness
  • Poor sleep
  • Low energy

Possible Causes

  • Sitting for long periods
  • Lack of exercise
  • Poor sleep routine
  • Unbalanced diet

Prevention Tips

  • Move regularly throughout the day
  • Exercise consistently
  • Get enough sleep
  • Eat nutritious foods
  • Manage daily stress
